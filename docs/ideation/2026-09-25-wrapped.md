# Wrapped

> How do we give every friend their Plex year as a wrapped they open, share and come back to during the year, with a new aesthetic every year?

## Framing

**For whom.** The guests linked in Sensorr (plex.tv accounts linked by PIN, `apps/api/src/app/guests/guest.schema.ts`), plus me, each matched to a Tautulli user.

**Success looks like.**
- The first minute on a phone makes an impression.
- Friends post a card or a slide as a story, or send it to me.
- Friends reopen their link on their own during the year.
- It starts conversations between friends: "you watched 40 hours more than me".

**Hard constraints.**
- Access through a signed link per guest, revocable. Guests have no Sensorr login today: the API knows a single user, set by environment (`apps/api/src/app/auth/auth.service.ts:10`).
- Movies and series.
- The 2026 edition ships early December 2026, about ten weeks from now.
- Any web technique is allowed, WebGL, shaders, GSAP, audio. The page stands apart from the app, and its look does not follow `DESIGN.md`.
- Data comes from the Tautulli instance that already runs next to Plex. Sensorr stores no watch history today, and nothing in the repo mentions Tautulli.
- What the repo already has: `framer-motion` 11 (used only by `libs/ui/src/atoms/Drawer/Drawer.tsx`), `libs/palette` which extracts a palette from a poster, TMDB metadata in Mongo (genres, runtime, countries, languages, collections; no cast or crew, the front asks TMDB for those).

**Already tried.** cinetropes designed a narrative Wrapped, live during the year and frozen at year end, with a shareable story card (`cinetropes/docs/brainstorming/brainstorming-session-2026-04-09.md:47-98`). It was never built: it depended on everything else and was pushed to the last wave.

**Why now.** A December 2026 edition.

**Left open.** How many plays a friend has in a year, and how many guests are linked. Both need a Tautulli sample and a look at the guests collection.

**What comes back everywhere.** Stories in 9:16 as the format. One template per edition fed with each person's data. An archetype or personality. A minimum-activity threshold. A lighter all-year view beside the yearly one. On the data side, Tautulli is the source for every Plex tool, and `rating_key` is not a stable identity.

**Not found.** No per-individual generative data art by the artists cited: their work is about groups, or themselves. No measured mobile budget from a wrapped team. No published accessibility review of a web wrapped. No documented way to change aesthetic from one edition to the next. No exact definition of `watched_status` values. No Tautulli retention policy.

## Ideas

Techniques: cross-pollination with cinema itself, senses and gestures, a day in the life (22 h, the busiest hour on the server). Ideas marked ★ are the ones I picked.

1. ★ The wrapped is a film: studio card, opening titles, acts, end credits rolling the titles watched.
2. A cinema ticket per friend, perforated, with seat, showing and hours: the share card.
3. ★ A 30-second trailer of your year, cut from the TMDB backdrops of your films.
4. ★ Your year as a movie barcode: one band per film, colour from its poster, in order.
5. ★ A festival poster made for you, "Festival de Thomas 2026", your top 10 as the official selection.
6. ★ A theatre programme, Cinémathèque style, with your cycles: "the Nolan month", "the Korean series summer".
7. A clapperboard opening each act, with scene, take and date.
8. ★ Your year's awards: Palme, Grand Prix, Jury Prize, given to your films by your own stats.
9. ★ Opening the link dims the room: black screen, a projector lights up, film grain comes in.
10. Until the first tap, a 35 mm projector hums in a loop; the tap starts the music.
11. Tearing your ticket with a swipe; the stub reveals the first figure.
12. Each slide takes the palette of the poster it talks about, with a colour crossfade between films.
13. Hours as something concrete: "297 h is The Godfather 138 times in a row".
14. A clock of your evenings: a 24 h dial where plays pile up like film traces.
15. The curtain half open during the year, opening a little more each month, wide open in December.
16. During the year, "we kept you a seat": last month's ticket and what changed since.
17. A rank that climbs like a queue for a showing: "you moved from row 12 to row 5".
18. End credits in free scroll, with a hidden post-credits scene: the film nobody else on the server watched.
19. ★ Your film age, from the mean release year of your films.
20. ★ Your world map: production countries as passport stamps.
21. ★ Your longest night: the evening with the most episodes back to back, and the time of the last one.
22. ★ The film you rewatched the most.
23. ★ Your drop of the year: the series left at episode N, from `percent_complete` and the next episode never started.
24. ★ Your decade, genre and country, as three cards to flip.
25. ★ Your share of films nobody else on the server started.
26. ★ Your year curve, month by month, with its peak named: "October, the slasher month".
27. ★ Your viewer archetype, from three axes: volume, variety, series against films.
28. ★ Your firsts: the first film of 2026, the first series started.
29. ★ Each edition is named after a film movement: 2026 Nouvelle Vague, 2027 Technicolor, 2028 Giallo.
30. ★ Each edition takes its palette from the server's most-watched poster of the year.
31. During the year the wrapped is a reel being edited, some shots still in black and white.
32. Closing on 1 December: the final cut is printed, then frozen.
33. ★ Past editions hang as posters in a cinema corridor.
34. ★ A teaser in November, "your film opens on 1 December", with a countdown.

## Directions

### Direction A, the screening
Stories in 9:16, tap to advance, in Spotify's line. Watched like a film: the room dims (9), acts, awards at the close (8), end credits with the titles watched (1). Each edition named after a film movement (29). Shared object: the festival poster (5).

| Test | Result |
|---|---|
| Value | The first-minute wow, and a format everyone knows |
| Cost and hardest part | 10 to 15 slides to get right every year, with their edge cases |
| Difference | Weak in form, it is Spotify; strong in its cinema vocabulary |

### Direction B, the programme
An editorial scroll page, like a printed Cinémathèque programme: cycles (6), official selection, awards, figures set like a newspaper. Each edition changes layout and type. Shared object: a page of the programme.

| Test | Result |
|---|---|
| Value | Read and reread, fits "I come back in August" |
| Cost and hardest part | Sharing on mobile: a long page does not go into a story |
| Difference | Rare for a wrapped, common on Awwwards |

### Direction C, the portrait
One generative artwork per friend, built from the poster colours of their films in order (4, 12), shaped by hours, genres and evenings. Figures are a layer explored inside the artwork. The algorithm changes each edition.

| Test | Result |
|---|---|
| Value | An object nobody else has, strong for sharing |
| Cost and hardest part | An artwork that says something, not a pretty gradient |
| Difference | The research found no per-individual data art |

Against C: 58 687 posters show orange dominates (Follows), so portraits risk looking alike, and figures move to the background.

### The throwaway and the moodboard
Both A and B were built on my real 2026 data (branch `jetable/wrapped`, `12f5edf`). The programme was nice, the screening less so, and neither was bold enough. A moodboard of every Spotify edition 2016–2025 plus 3D / WebGL and collage references (`d8bb03f`, `tools/wrapped-jetable/moodboard.html`) got every edition ticked: what I like in Spotify is not one style, it is changing radically every year.

## Retained direction

**The programme, bold**, chosen on 2026-09-26. The programme page carries the whole year; each edition breaks with the previous one in look, and cinema stays the raw material.

- Each guest's signed link opens a programme page, open all year, filled month after month, frozen on 1 December.
- The structure stays from year to year: cycles, year curve, awards, anonymous rank.
- The look breaks every year: 3D, WebGL, collage, type, anything goes.
- Posters, backdrops and film colours are the raw material.
- Each section exports as a 9:16 card, rendered server side, to share.

### Hypotheses to validate
- [ ] A scroll page can carry a bold, award-level look on a phone without the WebGL crashes reported on iPhone — tested by one section of the chosen 2026 look, 390 px, on a real iPhone, ten minutes of use.
- [ ] A 9:16 card per section is what friends share — tested by sending three cards to three guests and watching whether they post or forward one.
- [ ] Tautulli history, deduplicated and capped, gives the same figures as Tautulli's own screens — tested by comparing my 2026 hours and plays against Tautulli's user page.
- [ ] Guests match Tautulli users by email — tested by counting matches between the `guests` collection and `get_user`.
- [ ] The 2026 edition fits in ten weeks — tested by the contract's size once cut into slices.

### Smallest version
The 2026 edition: Tautulli import and stats in the Sensorr API, one signed link per guest, the programme page with its four sections in one bold look, and a shareable card for at least one section. It tests whether friends open, reread and share.

### What we don't do
- Stories as the main surface — the screening was built and did not convince.
- Adopting Wrapperr, Plex Rewind or Plex-Wrapped-for-Tautulli — tried, rejected on looks.
- Named rankings between friends — anonymous ranks only, nobody sees another person's history.
- Email delivery in this pass — decided later.
- A wrapped for Tautulli users who are not guests — figures use the whole server, access is for guests only.
- Calling Tautulli from the browser — no CORS on GET, and the API key would ship in the bundle.
- A signature gesture repeated every year — not one of the constants; breaking each year is.
- Production countries — Tautulli does not return them; needs TMDB, left for later.

### Open questions
- The 2026 look: chosen in design with two or three directions shown on real data.
- How many guests are linked today, and how many match a Tautulli user.
- Whether the 1 December freeze also freezes ranks, or ranks keep moving until 31 December.
- April 2025 has no history row: cause unknown, it matters for the 2025 archive only.

## Decisions

- 2026-09-25 · Build, do not adopt. Wrapperr, Plex Rewind and Plex-Wrapped-for-Tautulli exist and are maintained, but I tried them and find them ugly; Plex-Wrapped-for-Tautulli is passable, not my style. The bar is Spotify Wrapped: aesthetic, pro, pleasant, well thought out, artistic. Their Tautulli handling stays readable as reference.
- 2026-09-25 · Compute on the whole Tautulli history (100 users, 79 active in 2026), but only Sensorr guests get access to a wrapped. Server-wide figures feed the ranks.
- 2026-09-25 · Delivery by email, designed later. Out of this ideation.
- 2026-09-25 · Comparisons are anonymous ranks only ("top 5 % of the server", "3rd in hours"). Nobody sees another person's history.
